Dakly | Zero-Setup Headless Transactional Email Engine for Developers

Turn free Gmail and Google Workspace accounts into production-grade transactional SMTP relays. Zero monthly subscriptions, zero DNS records, and automated multi-sender failover pool.

Key Capabilities & Architecture

  • Multi-Gmail Failover Pool: Seamlessly cascade sends across backup Gmail accounts when the 500 emails/day threshold is reached.
  • Smart Warm-up & Rate Pacing: 14-day progressive schedule and leaky-bucket queue prevent spam flagging.
  • Sender Aliases Support: Send dynamically from custom domains configured in your Google account.
  • Real-time Telemetry: Track email open rates and link clicks with HMAC-signed webhooks.
  • Automatic Bounce Guard: Hard bounces (550 Mailbox Not Found) are automatically suppressed to protect sender reputation.
  • Military-Grade AES-256-GCM Security: Google App Passwords encrypted at rest with unique initialization vectors.

Quickstart with Node.js SDK

npm install @dakly/sdk

import { Dakly } from '@dakly/sdk';
const dakly = new Dakly('YOUR_API_KEY');

await dakly.sendOtp({
  to: 'user@example.com',
  otp: '849204',
  expiresInMinutes: 10
});

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Dakly completely free?

Yes. Dakly is MIT-licensed and connects to standard free Gmail accounts with App Passwords. No credit card or monthly plan required.

How do I bypass the 500 emails/day limit?

By connecting multiple backup Gmail accounts in your Dakly dashboard. Dakly automatically cascades traffic across all accounts in your pool.

Can I send emails from support@mycompany.com?

Yes. Add your custom email alias in Google Account settings as a 'Send mail as' alias, and Dakly will dynamically route sends using that alias.
